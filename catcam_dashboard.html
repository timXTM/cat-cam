📷 Camera mounting & image orientation

Choose how the camera sensor is physically mounted. CatCam will correct the preview and newly captured photos to appear upright. This does not rotate the camera physically.

If the image appears sideways or upside down, try the opposite sideways option or the advanced override. The settings apply to new captures only; existing photos remain unchanged. The motion ROI remains attached to the same physical area of the sensor.

Live preview appears when Settings is open.
ⓘ About camera mounting and rotation

Camera modules have a native sensor orientation. Software rotation corrects the output, but portrait versus landscape framing depends on physically mounting the camera. The motion ROI is stored in native sensor coordinates and transformed to match the live view.
